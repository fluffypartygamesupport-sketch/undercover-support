Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Undercover is designed to be played locally without creating an account.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share personal information through the app. The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services.

Game settings and progress may be stored locally on your device so the app can remember your preferences. This information is not transmitted to us.

The app offers in-app purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple may process purchase and transaction information according to Apple’s own privacy policy. The app may use purchase status information supplied by Apple to unlock purchased content and restore purchases.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app’s features or data practices change. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
