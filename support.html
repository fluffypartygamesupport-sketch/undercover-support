Frequently asked questions

How do I restore an in-app purchase?

Open the game’s purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. You will not be charged again.

Does the game require an internet connection?

Normal gameplay works offline. An internet connection may be required when purchasing or restoring in-app purchases through Apple.

Does the game collect personal data?

We do not operate user accounts, advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How many people can play?

Undercover supports 3–10 players sharing one device.
